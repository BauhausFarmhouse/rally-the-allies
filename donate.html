What is flashmob messaging?

This tactic relies on the fundamentals of marketing, where all marketing assets and surrounding branding exist to encourage an audience to read or absorb a message. The number of exposures to these elements informs the quality of memory–and connection to the message.

A flashmob is a force multiplier for messaging

  • 100% of donations are used to cover the cost of printing shirts.
  • Volunteers distribute shirts for free in mass at protests and civic events.
  • Messaging becomes prominent for all event attendees.
  • Attendees given free shirts will continue to promote messaging with continued use.
